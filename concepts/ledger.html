The numbers are only half of the ledger.

A product that clears every financial test still has to pass the questions a brand would ask. These are checked before the first order and again before every reorder.

RefCriterionWhat we checkWhen
L1Product suitabilityCondition, packaging, expiration windows, hazmat status and whether the item can be prepared correctly by our providers.Before PO
L2DocumentationInvoices, authorization letters and specifications that establish a clean chain of custody from the brand.Before PO
L3Channel permissionWhether the brand allows marketplace sales at all, on which channels, and under what pricing policy.Before PO
L4DemandSales history, seasonality and the number of existing sellers, so order sizes match real customer demand.Every reorder
L5Full landed costUnit cost plus freight, prep, marketplace fees, storage and the cost of the capital tied up in stock.Every reorder
L6Inventory performanceSell through, returns and customer feedback after launch. Weak performers are not reordered.Every reorder
