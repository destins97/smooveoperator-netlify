## Protected identity

The exact primary slogan is Wholesale, made Smoove. Preserve the initial capitals, comma and final period. Use Barlow Condensed 700 for Wholesale, made and the existing Yellowtail 400 for Smoove. The hero and the protected Smoove Operator wordmark use the same gold gradient. Do not generate a replacement logo.

## Direction

Recovered from aeec63e, built on the accurate content and functional baseline at 5a2ff52. Large condensed typography, warm-black material depth, a source-to-customer route, mechanical gold split-flap details and an operating-principles board give the business a recognizable operational identity. The typography is intentionally quieter in body copy and navigation.

No military styling, scanlines, terminal colors, fake telemetry, stock warehouses, public buying formulas or claims of existing scale. Texture is subtle local SVG grain and a low-contrast flap pattern. It is atmosphere, not data.

## Composition

The hero headline spans the desktop grid; the script payoff balances plain-English explanation and a supplier Fit Check CTA below the first line. Below 900px, the text and explanation stack. At narrow mobile sizes the slogan occupies three deliberate lines, never a narrow six-line column.

The route is five stations on desktop, a continuous vertical sequence on phones. Each station identifies its responsible party before revealing optional detail. The gold Evaluate tile identifies SmooveOperator's commercial responsibility, not an invented shipment status.

The principles board uses real ordinal numbering and four public principles. Native details reveal what suppliers can share in a first conversation. It does not expose private purchase thresholds or calculate hypothetical orders.

The business-profile preview is live HTML describing the actual linked resource. Subtle paper layering gives depth without implying another credential. The downloadable PDF is a separate one-page reference, not a screenshot.

## Interaction and access

Keep native details and full readable content without JavaScript. The route line draws once when it enters view; no endless motion, hidden text reveals or scroll hijacking. Reduced motion disables animations and transitions. No new runtime dependencies.

Preserve visible keyboard focus, Escape menu closure, input labels, required-field messages, duplicate-submit prevention, retained answers on failures, success focus, honeypot and native POST fallback. Supplier Fit Check is one continuous form, with optional product fields in a disclosure.
