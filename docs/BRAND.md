# SmooveOperator brand

## Positioning and audience

SmooveOperator is an independent marketplace commerce and distribution business. The primary audience is a brand or supplier account manager evaluating whether a conversation is worthwhile. The site should explain the business within 15 to 30 seconds and make supplier contact easy.

The focus is documented sourcing, product evaluation, purchasing, inventory decisions, third-party preparation and marketplace fulfillment coordination. Direct relationships are sought with brands, manufacturers, wholesalers and authorized distributors. Amazon FBA is an important channel, not the company identity. Repeat purchasing depends on demand, availability and economics.

## Identity

The protected identity is the Yellowtail cursive wordmark in Lamborghini gold `#FFC000` with a neon glow, on absolute black `#000000`. The home statement is the locked slogan “Wholesale, made Smoove.” Compressed Archivo capitals supply headings; normal-width Archivo supplies reading text and controls. All fonts are locally hosted. Black and gold dominate with near-white and muted gray copy.

## Visual expression

Use open columns, compressed capital headlines, square controls and thin structural rules. The homepage opens with long-exposure gold light trails and draws the five-stage route as the visitor scrolls. The signature diagram follows the product journey through native disclosures, with responsible parties always visible. It explains coordination without implying owned physical infrastructure. Supplier inquiry is one continuous form with optional product details.

Gold identifies actions, emphasis and the route. The wordmark keeps its neon glow. The light trails pause offscreen, stop after 30 seconds, offer a pause control and render a still frame under reduced motion.

## Voice and fact boundaries

Write in specific, calm, plain language. Lead with business purpose and explain roles. Distinguish sought relationships from established accounts, and repeat-purchase evaluation from purchasing commitments.

California seller's permit on file is an approved public fact. Resale documentation is exchanged directly during account applications. Do not publish its number, a personal name, home address, government ID, legal entity type, private email or phone. Do not invent partners, authorization, history, warehouses, scale, volume, performance or testimonials.

Keep Amazon FBA in operational context. Marketplace names do not imply affiliation, endorsement or authorization. An inquiry creates no purchase order or agreement. The archived vending business is outside scope. Do not use em dashes or en dashes in public copy.

## Accessibility and review

WCAG 2.2 AA is the target. Keep content readable without JavaScript, visible focus, semantic headings, native disclosures, labels and textual status feedback. Verify at 375, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px. These are requirements, not a claim that a release passed review.

Business-specific legal obligations require owner or counsel review. Publication does not constitute legal validation. [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) governs business truth; [Testing](TESTING.md) records verification.
