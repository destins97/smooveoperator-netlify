# Surface brief: smoove-operator.com (whole marketing site)

Scope: home, /suppliers/ (Fit Check + FAQ), /profile/ (reseller profile + PDF), /contact/ alias, privacy, terms, thank-you, 404. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: a brand, distributor or authorized wholesaler who received SmooveOperator's account application or outreach and is deciding if the reseller is legitimate and safe. Action: send a structured supplier introduction (Fit Check). Proof: written buy rules, commitments, CA seller's permit on file (no number). No invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Wholesale as a well-run station: the path a product travels, the rules every order passes and who handles each step are posted like departures, plain and on time. Replaces the prior "Gold Standard" banknote world (user picked Departures on the direction board, 2026-09-24). Refuses the flat dark SaaS template and the cliché Gatsby black-and-gold.
OWN-WORLD: Black stock #060605, board #0f0e0b, flap tiles with a hinge line, gold #D9B24C with highlight #F6DE8D and shadow #8A6B22, bone #ECE5D2, muted #A79E86, rules #2B261B. Barlow Condensed (600/700, italic 600 for step numerals) for headlines, boards, labels and buttons, uppercase; Barlow for reading; Yellowtail only for the gold script logo and the word "smoove.". Square buttons and switches. Components: route line (stations on a transit line, gold car), rules board (rule numeral flap, rule, live status column), flap digits for the sample math, timetable grids, manifest-style profile sheet, dashed open choices that print solid when chosen. Gold marks one claim per page.
STORY: Visitor reads "Wholesale, made smoove." and the one-sentence lede, watches the car travel the route (who handles each step), runs a sample listing through the rules board (when the marketplace sells it, Rule i's status reads "Marketplace is selling. Skip this listing." on a gold fill and the other rows tone down), checks commitments, sees the real profile, then starts the Fit Check, whose progress is its own three-station route.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: condensed uppercase "Wholesale, made" with gold script "smoove." beneath, around 120px desktop. Right: one-sentence lede in bone, primary gold "Start the Fit Check", outline "Read the buy rules", permit line. Behind the right half, a quiet empty flap board as texture. Sticky header with the gold script logo and gold CTA.
FORM: Departures board (user pick from the direction board; roll seed bb4158a1, degraded roll plus catalog challengers fetched directly). Kept from challengers: motion with mass (VU meter bridge), committed choices print solid (sewing pattern envelope).
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The route line is the one authored moment: a gold car travels station to station when the route comes into view, each dimmed-but-readable station lighting as it arrives. The sample listing updates each rule's status live and the math as flap digits (single flap per changed digit, no random cycling). Steps never flip. Motion grammar: cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1) for travel, 180/360/600ms, reduced motion shows the finished line.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
