# Surface brief: smoove-operator.com (whole marketing site)

Scope: home, /suppliers/ (Fit Check + FAQ), /profile/ (reseller profile + PDF), /contact/ alias, privacy, terms, thank-you, 404. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: a brand, distributor or authorized wholesaler who received SmooveOperator's account application or outreach and is deciding if the reseller is legitimate and safe. Action: send a structured supplier introduction (Fit Check). Proof: written buy rules, commitments, CA seller's permit on file (no number). No invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Wholesale credibility shown as security printing: the page is an engraved instrument of trust, with the rules printed on it like the fine print on a banknote. Refuses the category default of a flat dark SaaS template with icon cards and neon glow.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black stock #050504 with a panel #0e0d0a, brushed-foil gold #D9B24C with highlight #F6DE8D and shadow #8A6B22, bone text #EFE8D6, muted #A99F86, hairline rules #2d2715. Components: the engraved note (guilloche rope border, rosette corners, microprint, latent seal) holding the four rules, perforated stamp-sheet grids, a route line with diamond nodes, a stacked document preview, lowercase-roman numerals, foil-filled script wordmark, underline-only and ruled form fields, square gold buttons and switches. Gold marks one claim per page. Bodoni Moda display, Manrope body, JetBrains Mono for rule codes and numerals, Yellowtail wordmark only.
STORY: Visitor learns what SmooveOperator is in one sentence, sees the physical path (source, prepare, fulfill, reach) and who does each step, reads the four buy rules on the engraved note and runs a sample listing through them (toggle Rule 1, move the demand sliders) to see order sizing protects their price, checks commitments, sees the real profile, then completes the Fit Check.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: Bodoni headline "Wholesale, handled smoove." at ~110px desktop with foil script "smoove.", one-sentence lede in bone, primary gold button "Start the Fit Check" (the one label for /contact/ everywhere) plus outline "Read the buy rules", permit seal line in bone at 14px or more. Right half: a live guilloche rosette canvas bleeding off the right edge, reacting to pointer. Sticky thin header with foil wordmark and gold CTA.
FORM: Security-printing / engraved certificate, fused with the incumbent black-and-gold brand (the user chose the canon-adjacent card). Seed key 764057ef, kind canon.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The guilloche rosette's stacked plates turn slowly and lean toward the pointer by depth; the one authored moment is the note engraving its frame clockwise and printing the four rules in; the sample listing updates each rule's verdict and the note's result live, and Rule 1 stamps SKIPPED; the latent seal glints as it scrolls past. Motion grammar: Premium, cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1), 180ms/360ms/600ms, content visible by default.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
