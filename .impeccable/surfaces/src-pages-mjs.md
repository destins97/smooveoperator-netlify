# Surface brief: smoove-operator.com (whole marketing site)

Scope: home, /suppliers/ (Fit Check + FAQ), /profile/ (reseller profile + PDF), /contact/ alias, privacy, terms, thank-you, 404. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: a brand, distributor or authorized wholesaler who received SmooveOperator's account application or outreach and is deciding if the reseller is legitimate and safe. Action: send a structured supplier introduction (Fit Check). Proof: written buy rules, commitments, CA seller's permit on file (no number). No invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Wholesale credibility shown as security printing: the page is an engraved instrument of trust, with the rules printed on it like the fine print on a banknote. Refuses the category default of a flat dark SaaS template with icon cards and neon glow.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black stock #050504 with a panel #0e0d0a, brushed-foil gold #D9B24C with highlight #F6DE8D and shadow #8A6B22, bone text #EFE8D6, muted #A99F86, hairline rules #2d2715. Components: engraved ruled ledgers, a double-ruled certificate panel for rules, lowercase-roman step numerals, foil-filled script wordmark, underline-only form fields, square gold buttons. Bodoni Moda display, Manrope body, JetBrains Mono for rule codes and numerals, Yellowtail wordmark only.
STORY: Visitor learns what SmooveOperator is in one sentence, sees the physical path (source, prepare, fulfill, reach) and who does each step, reads the four buy rules and plays with the My Slice calculator to see order sizing protects their price, checks commitments, then completes the Fit Check.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: Bodoni headline "Wholesale, handled smoove." at ~110px desktop with foil script "smoove.", one-sentence lede, primary gold button "Open a supplier conversation" plus outline "Read the buy rules", permit seal line. Right half: a live guilloche rosette canvas bleeding off the right edge, reacting to pointer. Sticky thin header with foil wordmark and gold CTA.
FORM: Security-printing / engraved certificate, fused with the incumbent black-and-gold brand (the user chose the canon-adjacent card). Seed key 764057ef, kind canon.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The guilloche rosette slowly turns and its bands tighten toward the pointer; the buy-rules panel draws its engraved border on first view; My Slice calculator updates a foil-rendered order range live. Motion grammar: Premium, cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1), 180ms/360ms/600ms, content visible by default.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
